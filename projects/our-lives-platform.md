# Our Life on the Daily

**One line:** Household coordination platform prototype — shared scheduling, task lists, and fast voice-note capture for a two-person household.

## Overview

An early-stage full-stack prototype for coordinating shared household logistics between two people: schedules, chores, grocery lists, home-improvement planning, and quick voice-note capture, unified behind per-person profiles.

## Engineering highlights

- Dual-profile architecture with independent local login/passcodes per person.
- Fast voice-note capture pipeline that routes captured notes into the correct list (groceries, tasks, projects, appointments, medicine) rather than dumping everything into one inbox.
- Structured "big projects" tracker with wants/needs, location, planning steps, status, and budget notes — a lightweight project-management data model built from scratch.
- Shared-calendar concept spanning work schedules, meetings, and bill reminders.

## Stack

TypeScript, Vite, React (`.env`-based config, ESLint), local browser persistence for the prototype phase.
